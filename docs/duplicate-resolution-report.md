## 1. Executive Summary

During the initial phase audit of the BorrowBuy database, redundant product listings were detected—primarily generated during previous test runs and simulated multi-unit inventory injections (e.g. `Sony FX3 Cinema Camera Kit` with dynamic timestamp IDs, `Phase 5 Drone Kit`, and multiple `(Unit X)` / `(Set X)` entries).

In strict adherence to the **Data Safety Rules**:
1. **Zero Data Deletion**: No historical booking, payment, transaction, or review records were deleted.
2. **Historical Integrity**: Every product associated with historical booking records has had its lifecycle relations preserved intact.
3. **Marketplace Sanitization**: Redundant duplicates were deactivated (`listingStatus = INACTIVE`), removing them from active marketplace queries, category explorers, and user searches, while keeping all foreign key dependencies valid.
4. **Timestamp Purification**: Timestamp-based product names generated by automated tests were sanitized to human-readable studio identifiers (e.g. `Sony FX3 Cinema Camera Kit (Studio Rig)`).

---

## 2. Duplicate Detection Methodology

Product records were audited using multi-factor matching:
- **Normalized Product Name** (stripping timestamps, unit suffixes, and whitespace)
- **Brand & Model Identifiers**
- **Category & Subcategory Mappings**
- **Owner Relationship & User Role**
- **Image URL & File Hash Comparisons**
- **Historical Relationship Counts** (Active & completed bookings, payment transactions, review tokens)
